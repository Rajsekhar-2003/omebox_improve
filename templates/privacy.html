Please do not upload confidential or highly sensitive information such as passwords, financial credentials, or another person's private data.

1. No Registration Required

OmeBox does not require an account, name, email address, or phone number for its basic sharing features. However, using any website involves technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and server request data, which our hosting provider and other service providers may process. Using OmeBox without registration therefore does not mean that no information is processed.

2. Information We Process

A. Uploaded Text and Files

When you share text or upload a file, we store the submitted content temporarily so it can be retrieved through its link, QR code, or access code. If you set an optional password, it is used only to restrict access to that item.

B. Technical Information and Server Logs

Our servers and hosting provider may process:

  • IP address and connection information.
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system.
  • Request timestamps and requested URLs.
  • Error logs and security-related information.

This is used to operate the service, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the systems involved.

C. Contact Information

If you email us, we process your email address and the contents of your message to respond to your inquiry, investigate a complaint, or handle a removal request.

D. Advertising and Cookie Data

Advertising partners, including Google, may collect or receive identifiers such as cookie IDs, IP address, and device and browser information when ads are shown. See Section 5.

3. Temporary Storage and Data Retention

Supported sharing features offer expiration options (for example 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, or 7 days, depending on the feature). After the selected period, the service is designed to delete the corresponding content automatically. Deletion may take additional time, and technical logs or records may be kept separately where necessary for security, operational, or legal reasons. We do not guarantee that content is erased from every system at the exact moment of expiry.

To request removal before the scheduled expiry, email omebox24@gmail.com with the relevant sharing link or code. We review legitimate requests and act where reasonably possible.

4. Security

OmeBox uses HTTPS/TLS to encrypt data between your browser and the website. We take reasonable steps to protect stored content and our systems, but no website or transmission method is completely secure.

OmeBox does not provide end-to-end encryption. Content is encrypted in transit, but do not assume additional per-file encryption at rest unless explicitly stated.

Treat sharing links, QR codes, and access codes as private. Anyone who has a working link or valid code may be able to access the content, subject to any password you set.

5. Cookies, Local Storage and Advertising

Essential cookies and local storage

OmeBox may use essential cookies or browser local storage to operate the site, maintain security, and provide features such as the Web Notepad draft auto-save. Notepad drafts saved in local storage stay in your own browser until you clear them.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

OmeBox uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to OmeBox and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info, or in Europe at www.youronlinechoices.com.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Google's advertising technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by law, we ask for your consent through a consent management platform before ads or non-essential cookies that use personal data are enabled. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the privacy and cookie settings option on the site where available, or by clearing your browser cookies.

US state privacy laws

Residents of certain US states (including California) may have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. OmeBox does not sell uploaded content. To exercise a right, contact us (Section 14) or use the opt-out tools above.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how some features work.

6. How We Use Information

  • Provide temporary text and file sharing, and generate links, QR codes, and access codes.
  • Keep the website reliable, secure, and performant.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests, copyright notices, and removal requests.
  • Show advertising and measure its performance through our advertising partners.
  • Comply with applicable laws and lawful requests.

We do not sell or rent uploaded file or text contents. Information may be shared with service providers, advertising partners (for ad-related data only), or authorities where needed to run the site, provide enabled services, address security issues, or comply with law.

If you are in the EEA or UK, our legal bases are: performing the service you request, our legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention, compliance with legal obligations, and your consent for advertising cookies and similar technologies.

7. Third-Party Services

OmeBox relies on third parties for hosting and infrastructure, security, content delivery of libraries and fonts (such as Google Fonts, jsDelivr, and cdnjs), and advertising (Google AdSense). These providers may receive your IP address and device information when your browser loads their resources. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies. OmeBox does not control independent third-party sites or services.

8. Fair Use and Prohibited Content

You must use OmeBox lawfully and responsibly. You must not use the platform to:

  • Upload or distribute illegal content or material that violates another person's rights.
  • Share malware, malicious scripts, or files intended to damage or compromise systems.
  • Distribute copyrighted material without authorization.
  • Share child sexual abuse material or any content that exploits or endangers children.
  • Share adult, sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or otherwise harmful content.
  • Harass, threaten, impersonate, stalk, or dox others.
  • Distribute personal or confidential information without authorization.
  • Attempt to bypass security controls, interfere with the service, or abuse its infrastructure.

You are responsible for having the rights and permissions to upload and share your content. OmeBox may restrict, remove, or disable access to content when reasonably necessary to address reported violations, protect the service, or comply with law, and may preserve records or report matters to authorities where required or permitted. We do not guarantee that uploads are reviewed before they become accessible.

Responsibility disclaimer

You are solely responsible for content you upload and share. OmeBox does not actively monitor all content, but we remove reported illegal or prohibited material and cooperate with authorities where required or legally permitted.

Shared content pages are not intended as publicly indexed content. Ads are not intended to be shown alongside user-uploaded content.

9. Copyright Complaints and Abuse Reports

If you believe content shared through OmeBox infringes your copyright, violates your privacy, or is otherwise unlawful, contact:

omebox24@gmail.com — we respond to legitimate requests

Include the sharing URL or code, a description of the issue, and for copyright complaints, details identifying the protected work and the allegedly infringing material. We may ask for more information before acting. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in your report.

10. Children's Privacy

OmeBox is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK, or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information or inappropriate content, contact omebox24@gmail.com and we will review it.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data about you, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because OmeBox has no accounts, we may need your sharing link or code to locate relevant content.

You can also choose not to upload information, set a shorter expiry, add a password, manage cookies in your browser, and use the advertising opt-out tools in Section 5. Contact us to exercise your rights; we will assess requests under applicable law and our technical capabilities.

12. International Transfers

OmeBox may be accessed worldwide, and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, technology, legal requirements, or advertising practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date. Please review it periodically.
