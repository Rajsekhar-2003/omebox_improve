Send Files to Anyone Without an Account

Need to send photos, videos, documents, or other files? OmeBox turns your files into a shareable link and a 6-digit code that you can send to anyone.

Simply select your files, wait for the upload to finish, and share the generated link or code. You don't need to install an application or create an account. The recipient can open the link in a web browser and download the shared files.

A file transfer is organised into a data package. A package can contain multiple files with a combined size of up to 100 MB, and stays available until its expiry time (default: 3 days; you can choose anything from 15 minutes to 7 days).

How to Send Files Online

  1. Select your files: Click Share Files or drag and drop files into the upload area.

Security & Privacy: How OmeBox Protects Your Content

Sharing personal or work content online raises a fair question: who can see it? Here's how your content is handled.

Anonymous by Design

  • No account required — you never hand over an email, phone number, or password to share.
  • No advertising profiles from your shares — your text and files are not scanned for advertising.
  • Site analytics only — we use Google Analytics and Cloudflare to measure general site traffic. They don't receive the content of your shares. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Automatic Expiry

Every share has a built-in lifespan. When the expiry window ends, the content is deleted from OmeBox servers. You choose the window when creating the share:

  • 15 minutes — one-time codes or sensitive snippets
  • 1 hour, 24 hours — everyday sharing
  • 3 days — the default for file packages
  • 7 days — maximum, for larger transfers

Optional Password Protection

For sensitive documents, enable password protection. The recipient must enter the password before the file or text is displayed. The password is not embedded in the QR code or the share link.

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ We don't sell your data.
  • ❌ We don't show your shared content in search results.
  • ❌ We don't require you to sign in to download.
  • ❌ We don't keep expired shares "just in case."

If you're sharing work documents, we recommend following your organisation's internal data-handling rules.

Tips for Faster, Smoother File Sharing

  • Compress before uploading. A large folder of photos can often be zipped to fit under 100 MB.
  • Use strong Wi-Fi or a wired connection for uploads above 50 MB. Mobile data can be slow and may incur charges.
  • Send the 6-digit code through a different channel than the link — this adds a second layer of security.
  • Pick the shortest expiry that works. If the recipient is downloading now, 15 minutes is plenty.
  • Use QR codes for phone transfers. Scanning is faster than typing a code.
  • Keep the share tab open until the upload finishes. Closing it early can interrupt large transfers.
  • Check the expiry for other time zones. A 24-hour share sent at 11 PM may expire before the recipient wakes up — use a longer window.
