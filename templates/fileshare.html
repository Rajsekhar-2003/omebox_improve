Top Websites for Sharing Large Files Online (No Login Required)

If you need to transfer large files quickly and easily, several excellent online services allow you to share data without creating an account, phone number, or even logging in.

By OmeBox Blog Team  •  June 14, 2026  •  6 min read

Traditional email services often limit attachment sizes, making it difficult to send large projects, videos, design files, or software packages. File-sharing platforms solve this problem by allowing users to upload and transfer large files directly through a secure link — no signup needed.

These services are especially useful for: students sharing assignments, graphic designers working with Photoshop files, 3D artists using Blender or Maya, architects & engineers handling AutoCAD files, video editors with large media, and teams collaborating on datasets. Below we review the best free large file-sharing platforms that respect your privacy and time.

How to Share Large Files via Email

Most email providers limit attachment sizes to around 20–25 MB. If your file is larger than the allowed limit, you won't be able to attach it directly to an email. Instead, upload the file to a cloud storage platform or file-sharing service and send the download link to the recipient.

💡 Tip: If your file is under 25 MB, you can usually attach it directly to an email. For files larger than 25 MB, use cloud storage or a file transfer service to share download links instead of attachments.

Protect Your Sensitive Files Before Sharing Online

When transferring sensitive files through online platforms, take extra precautions to prevent unauthorized access. Follow these steps:

1. Password-protect files
Compress into ZIP/RAR and add strong password.
2. Share password separately
Via phone call, Signal, or different platform.
3. Delete after transfer
Remove files from temporary sharing services once delivered.
4. Avoid unprotected links
Encrypt before uploading.
Even trusted platforms can be breached. Encrypt your ZIP archives with AES-256 before sending.

Final Thoughts

Whether you need to send a large design project, a software package, a video file, or a collection of documents, these file-sharing services provide fast, convenient, and often free solutions. For temporary transfers, services such as ToffeeShare, FileTransfer.io, and TransferNow are excellent choices. For long-term storage and collaboration, cloud platforms like Google Drive, Dropbox, and OneDrive remain among the best options available.

Bottom line: No more email attachment limits. Pick a platform based on file size and privacy needs, and always password-protect confidential data.

Pro tip: Use a password-protected ZIP archive before uploading anywhere. This simple step adds an extra layer of protection even if the file link is accidentally shared with others.
