What Is OmeBox Text Sharing?

OmeBox is a free, browser-based text and code sharing tool. You paste what you want to share, choose how long it should exist, and OmeBox generates a shareable link, a 6-digit access code, and a QR code. There is no signup, no email, no app, and no account to manage.

Think of it as a Pastebin alternative built around temporary sharing. Every share expires, from 15 minutes to 7 days, and is then deleted. That suits one-off code snippets, meeting notes, configuration values, and quick reminders that shouldn't stay online permanently.

Whether you're a developer pasting a stack trace, a student sharing notes with a classmate, or a remote worker sending a link to a colleague, it takes a few seconds from any device.

Why Anonymous Text Sharing Matters

Text you paste into a chat app is usually stored on that company's servers for as long as the account exists. Anonymous, temporary sharing separates the act of sharing from your identity and limits how long the text lives.

OmeBox doesn't ask for an email or an account, and every share is stored with an automatic expiry and deleted when it ends. The site itself uses Google Analytics and Google AdSense, which may set cookies; they don't receive your shared text. See the Privacy Policy for details.

No web tool is perfect, so avoid sharing highly confidential information such as passwords, recovery phrases, or private keys. For everyday snippets, short-lived links are a lighter-weight option than chat or email.

Tips for Sharing Text Safely

  1. Use the shortest expiry that works. If the recipient is online now, choose 15 minutes or 1 hour.
  2. Send the code and the link separately. For example, the link by email and the 6-digit code by SMS.
  3. Don't paste secrets. Avoid API keys, private keys, recovery phrases, and passwords. If a link leaks, anyone with it can read the text until it expires.
  4. Clear your clipboard. After copying something sensitive, copy a harmless word to replace it, or use your OS clipboard-history tools to clear it.
  5. History is local. Removing an item from "Your Share History" only removes it from this browser. The share itself stays live until it expires.

Phone to PC

Mac to iPhone

Android to PC

No USB cable or account sync. Paste, generate, and enter the code on the other device.
