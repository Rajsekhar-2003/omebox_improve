📦 What is OmeBox?

OmeBox is a fast, simple platform for sharing files, text, code snippets, notes, and documents across any device. No accounts. No phone numbers. No registration.

Shared content is encrypted in transit over HTTPS, stored temporarily, and automatically deleted when the expiry time you choose runs out. You don't have to remember to clean up — it happens for you.

Whether you're moving files from your PC to your phone, sharing code with classmates, or sending documents between Android and iPhone, OmeBox keeps the process short.

Unlike traditional file-sharing platforms, OmeBox removes unnecessary steps: no signups, no complicated links, and no personal information required.

📖 The Story Behind OmeBox

Hi, I'm Rajsekhar Maity, the creator of OmeBox.

I graduated in Computer Applications, and the idea for OmeBox came from a problem I faced almost every day during university lab sessions.

We would write frontend and backend code in class, but when the session ended, transferring that work to our personal devices was surprisingly difficult. Most students relied on pen drives, WhatsApp, Telegram, or public text-sharing websites. These solutions were either inconvenient, required logging in, or generated long URLs that were easy to mistype and lose.

I wanted a platform where anyone could instantly share files, text, code, or documents without creating an account, simple enough to use within seconds. That idea became OmeBox.

I built OmeBox in 2024 for personal use and a small group of friends. Within a month, students across my university were using it regularly, and what started as a side project quickly grew to hundreds of users. As adoption grew, I invested in a custom domain and VPS infrastructure to improve performance and reliability.

The first version was built with Flask. As traffic increased, I ran into scalability challenges and occasional server crashes. Those experiences taught me the importance of robust architecture, and I migrated the platform to FastAPI and kept improving the infrastructure.

Today, OmeBox continues to grow while staying true to its original mission.

How We Protect Your Privacy

  • 🗑️

    Auto-Expiring Content

    Shared files and text are not stored permanently. Content expires automatically at the time you choose.

  • 🔍

    No Account Needed

    No accounts, phone numbers, or personal details are required to share.

  • 🔐

    Encrypted in Transit

    Uploads and downloads travel over HTTPS, so your content is encrypted between your browser and our servers.

  • 🚫

    No Content Profiling

    Your shared content is never analyzed, indexed, or used to target ads. See our Privacy Policy for what analytics and advertising data the site itself collects.

⚠️ For highly sensitive material (financial records, ID scans, password databases), use an end-to-end encrypted tool. No web-based sharing service, including OmeBox, replaces end-to-end encryption in those cases.
